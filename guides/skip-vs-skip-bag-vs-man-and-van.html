Skip vs skip bag vs man and van

The cheapest option is not always the lawful one. Whichever you choose, check the firm is a registered upper-tier waste carrier before they take anything away.

Comparison of skip hire, skip bags and man-and-van clearance

Best for Building work, mixed waste you will fill over several days, heavier loads such as soil or rubble in the right size skip. Smaller or slower jobs where you want to fill a bag on the driveway and have it lifted away later. One-off house, loft or garden clearances, furniture, or when you have no space to leave a container.

Skip hire

A skip is useful when you have a lot of mixed waste and somewhere safe to put the container. If it will sit on the public highway in Slough — the carriageway, footway or verge — Slough Borough Council says you need a permit from the Streetworks team. Permits are only issued to skip-hire companies registered with the council, so that the firm has the correct insurance and follows safety rules. Permits are issued for 14 days, and renewals can be requested for 7 days.

The council’s wording is important. “If you hire a skip, it is your responsibility to make sure it has been authorised.” You can email streetworks@slough.gov.uk to check that the skip you have hired is authorised, or to ask about registered skip-hire companies.

You still need to check the skip firm’s upper-tier waste carrier registration yourself. A council skip permit is not a substitute for that check.

Skip bags

A skip bag is a heavy-duty bag, often delivered flat, that you fill yourself. A collection lorry later lifts it away. This can suit driveways that cannot fit a traditional skip, or jobs that take a while to fill. Providers set their own rules on size, weight and prohibited items.

The collector is still taking your waste, so they must be a registered upper-tier waste carrier. National brands that serve an area should be checked in the same way as a local firm. Our Slough page includes one national skip-bag provider that, when last checked, had a live upper-tier registration and advertised collection in Slough.

Man and van, wait and load, or rubbish clearance

These services take the waste away on the day and do the lifting. They can be the right choice for furniture, a garage clear-out, or a house that has no room for a skip. They are also the option most often copied by unlicensed operators.

Treat a man-and-van collection like any other waste carrier: ask for the CBDU number, check it is upper tier and still valid, and keep a receipt. The Code of Practice says this includes a business that approaches you and offers to take your waste.

When you may not need a private firm

GOV.UK lists other lawful options, including your council’s bins, a special collection for large items, and taking waste to a household waste recycling centre. For Slough residents:

  • Slough Borough Council bulky waste collections can take up to five other bulky items at £19 each, with a £54 minimum charge, or up to two fridges, freezers or fridge-freezers at £29.70 each, again with a £54 minimum. Items are left outside and collected on your next scheduled domestic waste collection date after the booking has appeared in the council portal (at least three working days after booking). These figures were listed on the council’s page when we checked it on 19 November 2026 and may change — confirm on the official page before you book.
  • Chalvey Household Waste Recycling Centre is at White Hart Road, off Spackmans Way, SL1 2SF. The council says Slough and Windsor and Maidenhead residents can use it, and that you should bring proof of address and photo ID. Vans and pickup trucks are not permitted to use the HWRC; there is a 1.7 metre height barrier. Check opening hours and any temporary notices before you go. When we checked, the council also listed a temporary closure of the Langley recycling centre in Buckinghamshire until spring 2027.

Reusable furniture and appliances can often be sold, given away or donated, which keeps them out of the waste stream altogether.
