Quick answer

Central Reading, Caversham, Whitley and Southcote are Reading Borough Council. If your skip goes on the road, the skip firm applies for a Reading licence: £99.50 for up to 2 weeks, then £45 a week.

Key facts

  • 6 firms listed, each with a live upper-tier registration when we checked on 28 November 2026.
  • Reading skip licence: £99.50 for the first 2 weeks, £45 for each extra week, up to 4 weeks per licence.
  • Smallmead tip (RG2 0RP): book a slot. DIY waste is free up to 400 litres or 4 large items every 4 weeks, then £3.20 per 25-litre unit.
  • Town centre at Christmas: skips on 23 named town-centre roads get extra scrutiny from mid-November to mid-January.
  • Bulky items: Reading collects up to 3 items for £60, then £9.50 per extra item (5 max). Fridges and freezers are £55 each.

Watch out

Re-check the register before you book. Registrations expire and companies change. A listing here is only a starting point. Search the official Environment Agency register yourself, and read our licence-checking guide.

Which council is yours?

Reading Borough Council handles bins, bulky collections and skip licences inside the borough. The tip is run by re3, a partnership of Reading, Bracknell Forest and Wokingham councils.

Careful: a Reading postcode doesn’t always mean Reading Borough. Earley and Woodley are in Wokingham Borough, and Calcot, Theale, Purley and parts of Tilehurst are in West Berkshire, each with its own skip rules.

Skip licence fees for a skip on the road, from each council’s own website (checked 28 November 2026)

Reading Borough

£99.50 for up to 2 weeks, then £45 per extra week. Up to 4 weeks, then the firm must renew.

Wokingham Borough (e.g. Earley, Woodley)

£100 for 2 weeks, then £70 per extra week. Allow at least 5 working days.

West Berkshire (e.g. Calcot, Theale)

£62 plus £72 per week. Apply at least 5 working days before the skip arrives.

Show the full detail

Not sure which council you’re in? Use the GOV.UK council finder with your full postcode. Reading’s own pages say its skip licences only cover addresses inside the borough boundary.

In Wokingham, if parking restrictions apply, the skip also needs a parking dispensation at £53 a week, with 7 working days’ notice. In West Berkshire, the skip firm must hold £10 million public liability cover.

Reading bulky collections: book up to 5 items online. Upholstered seating (sofas, armchairs) must be booked and paid for separately from other items. Mattresses don’t count as upholstered seating.

Put items out before 6am on collection day, near where your bins go. The crew won’t collect from inside your home or garage, or from back gardens behind locked gates. Cancelling with less than 3 working days’ notice costs £12.30.

Could you take it to Smallmead instead?

Smallmead Recycling Centre is on Island Road, Reading RG2 0RP. It’s open every day, 8am to 6pm from October to March and 8am to 8pm from April to September.

You must book a 30-minute slot online (up to 7 days ahead) and bring proof that you live in Reading, Bracknell Forest or Wokingham. Traders and landlords can’t use it.

What Smallmead takes and what it charges, according to re3 (checked 28 November 2026)

Household and garden waste

Free.

DIY waste (rubble, tiles, wood, bathroom units)

Free up to 400 litres, or 4 large items up to bath size, in any 4 weeks. Say you’re bringing DIY waste when you book.

Over the free limit

£3.20 per 25-litre unit or per item. Plasterboard is £1.60 per 25 litres. Metal DIY items are free.

Asbestos

Up to 60kg within the free allowance, then £8.40 per 20kg. Book separately and phone re3 on 0800 988 3023.

Van or pickup

You need a commercial vehicle permit, added during booking. Box vans aren’t allowed.

Good to know

re3’s own guide puts a hatchback boot at about 400 litres. So one full boot of rubble uses your whole 4-week DIY allowance. For a bathroom or kitchen strip-out, a skip is usually simpler.

Show the full detail

Proof of address must be dated within the last 4 months, and for DIY waste it must show your full address. A driving licence, council tax bill, utility bill or bank statement all count. Photos of documents aren’t accepted.

Staff measure loose loads in 25-litre units (a cube about 29cm each side). Unused allowance can’t be carried over to a later visit. Children under 16 must stay in the car.

Refillable gas bottles cost £3 each; disposable cylinders are free. Bags of mixed hardcore and rubble are fine.

Christmas: re3 centres close from 4pm on Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve, and all day on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year’s Day.

If the skip has to go on the road

On your drive or private land, you don’t need a licence. On the road or pavement, your skip company applies to Reading Borough Council online and pays by card.

  • £99.50 covers up to 2 weeks. Each extra week is £45, so a full 4 weeks comes to £189.50.
  • One licence lasts up to 4 weeks. After that the firm must apply to renew.
  • The firm must upload its current public liability insurance.
  • Skips are not allowed on some town-centre roads at all, though special arrangements can sometimes be agreed.

Planning work in the town centre this winter?

From mid-November to mid-January, Reading gives extra scrutiny to skips on 23 town-centre roads, including Broad Street, Friar Street and Oxford Road. If the job isn’t urgent, the council may ask to delay it until mid-January.

Show the full detail

Reading calls this Christmas policy “Operation Everrt”. The full road list is: Oxford Road (Alfred Street to Broad Street), Broad Street, Butter Market, Chain Street, Cross Street, Dusseldorf Way, Friar Street, Greyfriars Road, Gun Street, High Street, Hosier Street, Kings Street, Market Place, Merchants Place, Minster Street, Queen Victoria Street, Queens Walk, St Mary’s Butts, Station Approach, Station Road, Union Street, West Street and Kings Road (to the Library).

On this page, J Mould says it arranges the permit at the council’s rate, Hemans says its prices include permits, and Brazil says a licence adds up to £100. Reynolds and Reading Recycling ask you to phone. Always ask whether the licence is in your quote.
