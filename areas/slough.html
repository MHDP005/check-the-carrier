If the skip will sit on a Slough road

Slough Borough Council requires a permit to place a skip on the public highway, including the carriageway, footway and verge. Permits are only issued to skip-hire companies registered with the council, last 14 days, and can be renewed for 7 days.

The council says it is your responsibility to make sure the skip has been authorised. Email streetworks@slough.gov.uk to check.

Council options first

For a few bulky items, the council bulky waste collection may be enough (£19 an item for most items, £54 minimum, as listed on 19 November 2026).

You can also take household waste to Chalvey Household Waste Recycling Centre, White Hart Road, off Spackmans Way, SL1 2SF, with proof of address and photo ID. Vans and pickup trucks are not permitted at the HWRC. Check hours and any temporary closures before you travel.

When we checked, the council noted that the Langley recycling centre in Buckinghamshire was closed until spring 2027.
