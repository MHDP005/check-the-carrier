Two councils, two jobs

Staines is in Surrey, not Berkshire, so the rules are different from our Windsor and Maidenhead pages, even though Windsor is only a few miles away.

Waste is split between two councils. Spelthorne Borough Council empties your bins and runs the bulky item collection. Surrey County Council runs the tips, which it calls community recycling centres (CRCs), and as the highway authority it is the council that licenses skips on the road.

Knowing which one to look at saves a lot of searching.

Can you take it to Shepperton tip instead?

The nearest Surrey tip to Staines is Shepperton Community Recycling Centre, Charlton Lane, Shepperton TW17 8QA. It is one of the Surrey sites that takes vans and construction waste, and one of only three (with Epsom and Guildford) that take asbestos.

Surrey’s free allowance for rubble and similar DIY waste is small, though, which is often what tips a renovation towards a skip. This is what Surrey County Council’s pages said when we checked on 24 November 2026:

What Shepperton Community Recycling Centre accepts and the limits that apply, according to Surrey County Council

Who can use it

Surrey residents. Bring proof of address on your first visit, such as a driving licence, this year’s council tax bill or a recent utility bill, and you will be given a resident sticker for your windscreen. No booking is needed. Pedestrians cannot use Shepperton.

Rubble, soil, plasterboard, tiles, concrete, bathroom porcelain

Free only up to the limit, and no more than once every seven days: two bags of up to 50 litres, or one large item up to 200 × 75cm, or one sheet of plasterboard up to 120 × 140cm.

Above that, £5.60 per bag or item, £12 per sheet of plasterboard and £50 for a loose carload, all including VAT and payable by Visa or Mastercard only.

Doors, kitchen units, worktops, radiators, insulation, fencing, sheds, flooring

Free in any amount when you bring them in a car. They are not accepted in a van.

Asbestos

Accepted at Shepperton, Epsom and Guildford only. Read the council’s asbestos page before you go.

Tyres

£5.00 including VAT per tyre or part tyre; bicycle tyres are free.

Going in a van, pickup or trailer

You need a van permit: £10 a year, up to 12 visits, one per household, household waste only, and you must apply for it yourself.

Rubbish someone else removes

If you hire a van with a driver to get rid of your waste, Surrey classes it as business waste, and Shepperton does not take business waste. A clearance firm needs its own licensed disposal route, which is why you should check its registration.

Opening times

8am to 4pm Monday to Friday and 9am to 4pm at weekends. Closed Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year’s Day, and closes at 2pm on Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve.

Show the full detail

In practice, a garage clear-out or a garden full of old fencing can often go to Charlton Lane in a car over a few weekends for nothing.

A kitchen or bathroom strip-out is different: the tiles, plasterboard and rubble will quickly go over the two-bag weekly allowance, and a skip, skip bag or grab lorry is usually simpler. Shepperton also has a Revive reuse shop on site if you have furniture worth passing on.

For a few large household items, Spelthorne’s bulky waste collection is run by Kingston Community Furniture and cost £90 including VAT for one to five items or £125 for up to eight when we checked, with a £10 discount for people on income support or pension credit.

It will not take garden waste, building rubble, kitchen cabinets, windows, baths, fixtures or fittings. Fridge and freezer collections were also suspended because of a national shortage of processing capacity, so take those to the tip for now.

If the skip has to go on the road

If there is no room on your drive or front garden, the skip will have to go on the road. Surrey County Council requires a skip licence for a skip on any part of the highway, including the pavement, a verge or the side of the road. A skip in your front garden or on your drive does not need one.

Only skip companies registered with Surrey County Council can apply, so this is something your skip firm does, not you. The council’s licence page says:

Show the full detail
  • A licence costs £139 for up to 28 days, and each 28-day renewal costs another £139 (also listed in the council’s highway fees and charges).
  • The council aims to issue it within two working days of a full application.
  • Suspending parking bays or bus stops, or temporary traffic lights, cost extra.
  • The council can add conditions on the skip’s position, size, lighting and markings, and can refuse a licence where the spot would be hazardous to traffic.

Ask every firm whether its quote includes the licence. On this page, B&K, HIPPO and Aaron say they apply for it; FJL says it only places skips on private land.
